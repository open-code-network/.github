# OCN Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

In the interest of fostering an open, welcoming, and rigorous engineering environment, **Open Code Network (OCN)** pledges to making participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual orientation.

---

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment for our engineering squads include:

*   **Professionalism & Respect:** Using welcoming and inclusive language. Respecting differing viewpoints and architectural approaches.
*   **Constructive Peer Review:** Offering and accepting critique gracefully. In our code reviews, we critique the code to build better systems, not the person who wrote it.
*   **Public Accountability:** Honoring commitments to our shared repositories, squads, and open-source timelines.
*   **Empathy:** Supporting newer builders and student engineers as they scale up to production-grade standards.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:
*   The use of sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
*   Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
*   Public or private harassment.
*   Publishing others' private information (such as a physical or email address) without explicit permission.
*   Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional engineering setting.

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all OCN GitHub organizations, repositories, communication channels (such as community Discord/WhatsApp servers), and in-person or virtual university chapter events.

---

## Enforcement & Reporting

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported by contacting the core maintainers or repository leads at **Open Code Network**. 

All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate administrative action (including code commit revocation and permanent community ban) in response to any behavior they deem inappropriate, threatening, or harmful.
